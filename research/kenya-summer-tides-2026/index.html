The Itinerary

Festival nights stay protected. Adventure gets stacked after the festival so nobody is waking up at 4:30 AM before a beach-party night.

Jul 1

Arrive, check into Billionaire, beach dinner

Land in Nairobi, connect to Malindi, get to Billionaire Beachfront Apartments, stock the kitchen, then do a soft landing at the beach bar or nearby Italian/seafood dinner.

Jul 2

Summer Tides Day 1

Late breakfast, pool, wristbands, first night at Lost Beach Club. Do not schedule a major daytime tour.

Jul 3

Summer Tides Day 2

Beach recovery day. Optional spa/tennis/pool at Billionaire, then back to the festival.

Jul 4

Summer Tides finale

Keep this day loose and social. Dinner early, festival late.

Jul 5

Watamu Ocean Safari + Mida Creek sunset dhow

Morning/early afternoon in Watamu Marine Park for reef, dolphins, turtles, manta/whale-shark luck, and marine-life spotting. At sunset, sail Mida Creek on a traditional dhow with drinks/snacks.

Jul 6

Tsavo East safari

Private day trip: 5:00 AM departure, Sala Gate, Aruba Dam/Voi River wildlife circuit, lodge/picnic lunch, evening return to Malindi.

Jul 7

Depart or recovery brunch

If flights leave late, do Billionaire pool, beach, or a slow brunch. If routing through Nairobi, leave plenty of connection buffer.

Jul 5 morning

Watamu Marine Megafauna Day

Book this as the whale-shark-inspired blue-water day: reef snorkeling, turtles, dolphins, possible manta/whale-shark luck, and July whale-migration energy. The cleanest premium path is Hemingways Watamu's marine association/snorkelling experiences; Blue Lilac is the more explicit whale-shark inquiry lead.

  • RealityJuly is a marine megafauna lottery, not a guaranteed whale-shark month. The operator should be able to pivot to dolphins, turtles, reefs, whale watching, scuba, or fishing.
  • Ask forPrivate 2-4 person ocean safari with outer-reef spotting if conditions allow.

Wild-card swaps

Scuba, Kitesurfing, Or Deep Sea Fishing

If the group wants water-sport chaos instead of a chill marine day, use Hemingways for scuba/kite/fishing or Che Shale for big-game fishing. July-September can bring Black Marlin, Sailfish, Wahoo, Kingfish, Giant Trevally, and Yellowfin Tuna.
