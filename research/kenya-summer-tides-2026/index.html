Recommended Itinerary

This keeps the festival nights protected and puts the long safari day after the music, when nobody has to rush back for a headline set.

Jul 1

Arrive in Malindi

Fly into Nairobi, connect to Malindi if possible, check in, buy water/snacks, and do a low-effort beach dinner.

Jul 2

Festival Day 1

Pick up wristbands early. Confirm the return taxi plan before entering the venue.

Jul 3

Festival Day 2

Keep daytime plans light: beach, pool, lunch, recharge.

Jul 4

Festival Day 3

Final festival day. Do not schedule safari or boat activities this morning.

Jul 5

Marine Park / Boat Day

Book a weather-dependent Malindi Marine Park snorkel or Sawa Sawa-style dhow excursion. Confirm marine-park fees, lunch, pickup, and cancellation policy.

Jul 6

Tsavo East Safari

Private day safari is feasible but long: about a 5:00 AM departure, roughly 3-3.5 hours each way, game drives, lunch, and late return.

Jul 7

Depart

Fly Malindi to Nairobi or transfer to Mombasa. Avoid tight same-day international connections if using road transfer.
