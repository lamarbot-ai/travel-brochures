Stay close to Lost Beach Club — not Watamu

Summer Tides is at Lost Beach Club in Malindi. I corrected the plan to prioritize Malindi / Lawford / Silversand / Casuarina / Marine Park stays and demoted Watamu to a day-trip, not the home base.

See venue-close shortlistExperiences guide

My updated lodging verdict

Book Haven Stays if you want the safest reviewed Malindi pick. It is $997 for the week, 2BR/2BA, 4.86/28 reviews, and says 3 minutes to the beach.

Book Dino Residence if price matters most and you can tolerate new-listing risk. It is $603 for the week, 2BR/2BA, and says “stone’s throw from the sea.”

Do not use Watamu as the main base for this festival. Watamu is great for the reef/coastal day, but it is a separate town roughly 20+ km / 25–35 min from the venue area in normal conditions.

Where to stay for Summer Tides

1. Best: Lawford / Silversand / Malindi Beach

Closest to Lost Beach Club. Lost’s own Instagram says The Lawford Hotel is right behind them. Prioritize anything clearly around Lawford, Silversand Road, or Malindi Beach.

2. Good: Casuarina / Billionaire / Marine Park

Still same south-Malindi coastal corridor; likely short taxi/Bolt. Better beach-resort inventory, but generally not as walkable as Lawford/Silversand.

3. Too far as home base: Watamu

Keep Watamu for the July 5 reef/ocean day. Do not commute from there every festival night unless intentionally accepting ride logistics.
