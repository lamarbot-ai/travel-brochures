Plan summary

The best option if the group is large and price-aware.

This plan gives up hotel services for lower lodging cost, more common space, and better nightlife access. It needs a stronger organizer: multiple hosts, multiple deposits, and a driver plan.

Why pick it

More beds, kitchens, living rooms, and better split economics for a 10-15 person group.

Meals

Food plan built around Woodbrook.

This itinerary deliberately uses Ariapita Avenue/Woodbrook for convenient meals and fewer taxis.

Breakfast

Apartment groceries: fruit, water, electrolyte packets, eggs, bread, coffee. Budget $8-$15 pp/day.

Casual meals

Food Square, El Pecos, pizza/jerk/doubles/barbecue options around Ariapita. Budget $18-$35 pp.

Sit-down meals

Veni Mange, Chaud-style One Woodbrook dining, hotel rooftop fallback. Budget $60-$110 pp with drinks.

Day-by-day

Complete itinerary.

This is the most social version: common-room pregames, flexible subgroups, and more spontaneous food options.

Wed Feb 3

Arrive + apartment setup

  • Two airport transfer waves to Woodbrook.
  • Unit walkthroughs, photo damage checks, grocery run.
  • Dinner: Food Square / Ariapita casual. Budget $35-$50 pp.
Thu Feb 4

Costume pickup + welcome lime

  • Costume/band logistics by subgroup.
  • Common apartment pregame with groceries.
  • Dinner: Veni Mange or casual split. Optional welcome fete.
Fri Feb 5

Culture day + first big fete

  • Queen's Park Savannah, Magnificent Seven, Botanic Gardens.
  • Rest at apartments.
  • Evening premium fete with pre-booked driver pickup.
Sat Feb 6

Soca Brainwash / split group day

  • Main group does Soca Brainwash if tickets secured.
  • Lower-cost subgroup does Woodbrook food crawl + Savannah events.
  • Late food near Ariapita, walk only if safe and close.
Sun Feb 7

Maracas + road prep

  • Private van to Maracas Beach, bake and shark lunch.
  • Back before dark; laundry/road bag prep.
  • Apartment dinner and early night.
Mon Feb 8

J'ouvert + Carnival Monday

  • Buddy-pair J'ouvert only; no solo departures.
  • Band road day or spectator route.
  • Recovery: apartment showers, ordered food, sleep.
Tue Feb 9

Carnival Tuesday

  • Full costume day.
  • Group photo slot before stage crossing.
  • Late-night Ariapita food for anyone still standing.
Wed Feb 10

Recovery and farewell dinner

  • Late brunch cooked in units or nearby cafe.
  • Pool/day-pass inquiry if apartments lack pool.
  • Farewell dinner at Chaud/Veni Mange/hotel rooftop.
Thu Feb 11

Checkout + airport waves

  • Clean units, take checkout photos, collect deposits.
  • Two airport transfer waves.
  • Keep one day-use room/unit if late flights cluster.
