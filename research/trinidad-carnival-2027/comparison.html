How to hit $3,000

  • 📍 Stay outside POS: Look at St. Augustine, Tunapuna, or San Juan. You'll use Maxi Taxis for $1-2 instead of private drivers.
  • 🎭 Backline Mas: Skip the 10-foot feather backpacks. Backline costumes are 50% cheaper and give you the same road access.
  • 🧊 Cooler Fetes: Choose "Cooler" events where you bring your own drinks in a bag/cooler. Ticket prices are lower (~$50-80).
  • 🌮 Street Food: Doubles, Bake & Shark, and Aloo Pies are the best food on the island and cost $1-$8 per meal.
