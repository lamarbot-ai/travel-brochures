Plan summary

The cleanest land itinerary if rooms are available.

Brix is the best hotel-centered option because it is close to Queen's Park Savannah and has the polish a mixed group will expect. This is the plan I would use for a core group that wants Trinidad proper, not the cruise.

Why pick it

Good location, hotel services, pool/rooftop, and easier coordination than apartment stacking.

Savannah edgeHotel supportPremium feel

Main risk

Carnival hotel pricing can be punishing, and room blocks may be limited. Hold rooms early with cancellable terms.

Flight model

Use ORD-POS via MIA, JFK, IAH, or PTY. Miami-POS has nonstop American/Caribbean Airlines; JFK-POS has nonstop JetBlue/Caribbean Airlines.

Day-by-day

Complete itinerary.

This is a high-comfort Carnival week with deliberate meals and enough recovery time to survive Monday/Tuesday.

Wed Feb 3

Arrive POS + hotel dinner

  • Group transfer POS to Brix.
  • Check in, buy water/snacks, confirm driver contact.
  • Dinner: hotel restaurant or Chaud-style nearby meal. Budget $65 pp.
Thu Feb 4

Costume logistics + Woodbrook night

  • Costume pickup/fitting and band wristbands.
  • Lunch: Food Square or quick Ariapita option.
  • Dinner: Veni Mange reservation if possible; optional welcome fete. Budget $120-$220 pp.
Fri Feb 5

Savannah orientation + premium fete

  • Walk/drive Queen's Park Savannah, Magnificent Seven, Botanic Gardens.
  • Pool reset at Brix.
  • Evening: one premium all-inclusive fete. Budget $250-$450 ticket.
Sat Feb 6

Soca Brainwash anchor day

  • Late breakfast, Soca Brainwash at St. Mary's if secured.
  • Recovery meal: hotel/room service or El Pecos-style quick food.
  • No second major event unless group is very high stamina.
Sun Feb 7

Maracas Beach reset

  • Private driver to Maracas Beach, bake and shark lunch.
  • Back by late afternoon for road prep.
  • Early dinner; pack road bags and assign buddy pairs.
Mon Feb 8

J'ouvert + Carnival Monday

  • Pre-dawn J'ouvert for committed travelers.
  • Monday wear / casual road day with band.
  • Return to Brix in waves. Simple hotel food after.
Tue Feb 9

Carnival Tuesday

  • Full costume and stage crossing.
  • Photo meetup before road disperses.
  • Late-night meal near hotel or ordered in. No complex plans.
Wed Feb 10

Recovery day

  • Sleep, pool, massages if available.
  • Late lunch at Woodbrook/One Woodbrook Place.
  • Farewell dinner: Chaud, Veni Mange, or hotel rooftop depending reservations.
Thu Feb 11

Depart Trinidad

  • Stagger airport transfers by flight group.
  • Late checkout/luggage hold.
  • Keep $40-$60 pp for airport food/transport buffer.
