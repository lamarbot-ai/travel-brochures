Plan summary

Choose this when capacity beats walkability.

Hyatt Regency Trinidad is not as Savannah-adjacent as Brix/Kapok, but a large hotel can be easier for 10-15 people, staggered arrivals, luggage, and room-block management.

Why pick it

Large hotel infrastructure, waterfront base, group-room potential, event/meeting capabilities, easier front-desk support.

Transport

Driver-first operating model.

This plan needs scheduled transport the way the cruise needs Barbados boarding. It should be treated as part of the itinerary, not an afterthought.

Airport

Two pre-booked airport waves on Feb 3; one luggage-hold/late-checkout plan on Feb 11.

Events

Pre-book outbound and return windows for Soca Brainwash, premium fete, J'ouvert, and Tuesday return.

Beach day

Use one private van for Maracas and leave early enough to avoid a late return before road days.

Day-by-day

Complete itinerary.

This schedule keeps the hotel as the calm base and limits spontaneous cross-town movement.

Wed Feb 3

Arrive + waterfront base

  • POS airport transfer waves to Hyatt.
  • Check in, group drink/briefing, confirm driver calendar.
  • Dinner: hotel/waterfront simple meal. Budget $60-$90 pp.
Thu Feb 4

Costumes + Woodbrook dinner

  • Driver loop for costume pickup and band registration.
  • Early evening Woodbrook dinner at Veni Mange/Food Square area.
  • Return together; no scattered taxis on first night.
Fri Feb 5

Savannah orientation + premium fete

  • Late morning Savannah/Magnificent Seven visit.
  • Hotel pool/rest.
  • Premium fete with fixed pickup window. Budget $250-$450 ticket.
Sat Feb 6

Soca Brainwash anchor

  • Hotel breakfast, leave as a group.
  • Soca Brainwash if secured; otherwise alternative all-inclusive.
  • Return to hotel, late room-service style recovery.
Sun Feb 7

Controlled beach reset

  • Private van to Maracas Beach.
  • Bake and shark lunch; back by 4 PM.
  • Road prep at hotel; no late night unless skipping J'ouvert.
Mon Feb 8

J'ouvert + Carnival Monday

  • Pre-dawn transport only for committed J'ouvert subgroup.
  • Band road day; hotel return windows every few hours.
  • Use hotel as command center for anyone dropping early.
Tue Feb 9

Carnival Tuesday

  • Full costume day.
  • Predetermined pickup point away from the densest traffic.
  • Hotel recovery meal after the road.
Wed Feb 10

Recovery + final dinner

  • Sleep, pool, spa/massage inquiries.
  • Optional Caroni Swamp or easy city tour for people with energy.
  • Farewell dinner at Hyatt or Woodbrook reservation.
Thu Feb 11

Depart

  • Staggered airport transfers.
  • Hotel luggage hold for late flights.
  • Settle driver tips and shared costs before airport.
